Se vienen cositas:
announcing our first book
author: TraTraTrax





The yearly tradition is here, our utilitarian bass-on-the-floor compilation 'no pare, sigue sigue' is on its fourth edition, and let us tell you... This one is quite special. Eighteen tracks, first-time collaborations, label staples, plus new exciting talent from the global south and beyond. A packing sharp percussive bomb, a techno ensamble, a revolving bass blender and a punch in the ears and la cadera. This is it, our club tools for the season.

Tracks for the sun-risen dancefloor, the midnight peaktime, the afternoon benders, the dembow aficionados, the sweaty boats or the drum addicts. From São Paulo to Mexico DF, Bogotá to NYC, Montevideo to Paris, Montreal to Milan: a snippet of the current state of sound design-fueled club music is contained within the four corners of this release. Enjoy the harvest and its power, nos vemos.
